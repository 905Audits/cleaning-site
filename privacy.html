Privacy

Privacy policy

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

When you request a quote or booking, we collect your name, email, phone number, service address, home details, optional photos, preferred dates, and any notes you share. Cleaner applicants share contact details and work information.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to prepare your quote, schedule and deliver your clean, process payment, answer questions, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Consent and marketing

By sending a request, you consent to us using your information for these purposes. We send promotional messages only if you opt in by checking the marketing box, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). You can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate, such as payment processing through Stripe, form delivery, and scheduling tools, and only as needed to provide our service.

Storage on your device

Our booking form saves your progress in your browser's local storage, so you don't lose your place. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data. We do not use advertising cookies.

Retention and safeguards

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we protect it with reasonable safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
