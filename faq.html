FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you book.

How is my price calculated?

Your estimate is based on the service, bedrooms, bathrooms, and size of your home. We confirm your final quote before anything is booked, so there are no surprises.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. We bring professional cleaning products and equipment.

Can I book within 72 hours?

Yes, turn on Urgent when you pick your time. An urgent fee applies and is confirmed in your quote.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free with 72 or more hours' notice, $60 within 2 days, and $85 on the same day. See our policies.

Which areas do you serve?

Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, and Cambridge.

What's the difference between a Standard and a Deep clean?

A Standard clean covers regular upkeep. A Deep clean adds detail work like baseboards, grout, build-up, window sills, and high dusting. Compare services.

Are add-ons included in my estimate?

Add-ons like inside the oven, fridge, and windows are à la carte, subject to assessment. We price them in your confirmed quote.

Do I need to upload photos?

No, photos are optional. They help us confirm your quote faster and more accurately.

Do I need to be home?

No. Choose how we get in when you book, such as a key, lockbox, or concierge, or let us know you will be home.

Can I book on a holiday?

Yes. Holiday bookings require prepayment, and a holiday premium applies.

Can you clean late at night?

Yes. Bookings after 10 PM have an after-hours premium, confirmed in your quote.

How do I pay?

By credit card through Stripe or by Interac e-Transfer. Payment details are shared with your confirmed quote.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. Recurring Standard cleans save 10% monthly, 12% every 2 weeks, and 15% weekly.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Please report any concern within 30 minutes of your clean being completed, so we can review it right away.

Are pets okay?

Yes, pets are welcome. Let us know about them when you book, and add pet hair treatment if you need it.

Still have a question? Contact us or start your booking.
