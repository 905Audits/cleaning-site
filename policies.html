Booking and cancellation policy

Clear rules, so everyone knows what to expect.

Quotes and booking

  • Prices on this website are estimates. Your final quote is confirmed before your booking is locked in.
  • Add-ons are à la carte, subject to assessment, and included in your confirmed quote.
  • Bookings are scheduled based on our availability. Times within 72 hours require an Urgent booking, and an urgent fee applies.
  • Bookings after 10 PM have an after-hours premium.
  • Holiday bookings require prepayment, and a holiday premium applies.

Cancellations and rescheduling

  • 72 or more hours' notice: free.
  • Within 2 days of your clean: $60 fee.
  • On the same day: $85 fee.
  • Late fees are non-refundable.

Refunds

  • Refunds are not provided once a clean has been completed. Refunds apply only to services not rendered.
  • Prepaid holiday bookings follow the cancellation terms above.

Concerns after your clean

Please report any concern within 30 minutes of your clean being completed, so we can review it while the details are fresh.

Jobs stopped partway

If a clean is stopped partway at your request, the time completed is charged at $75 per hour, per cleaner.

Payment

We accept credit cards through Stripe and Interac e-Transfer.

Pets and access

Pets are welcome. Please make sure we can safely access your home at the booked time and let us know about any pets.
