The basics of using our website and services.

Estimates and quotes

Website prices are estimates for typical homes. Your confirmed quote may differ based on size, condition, add-ons, timing, and access, and it is shared with you before your booking is confirmed.

Your booking

A booking is confirmed once you approve your quote. Please provide accurate details and safe access to your home. Our booking and cancellation policy forms part of these terms.

Our service

We bring our own supplies and equipment. We may decline tasks that are unsafe, such as work at heights, hazardous materials, or heavy lifting.

Payment is due as stated in your quote, by credit card through Stripe or by Interac e-Transfer. Holiday bookings require prepayment.
